## Sideline Background  
Sideline is a mobile app available on iOS, Android, and Web that adds a second phone number to your phone, keeping your personal number private and protected. With a separate contact list, dedicated voicemail, and custom ring and text tones, Sideline also keeps your professional and personal communications separate—so you don’t need to purchase a second phone. Sideline’s patented technology utilizes your existing carrier network for dependable call quality wherever you are, and integrates unlimited text messaging, voicemail, and additional features to manage calls and messages efficiently.

Originally developed to simplify, separate, and organize how professionals and individuals communicate, it has evolved to introduce privacy-focused features that help people protect their personal information and address modern calling and texting issues, like spam filtering that automatically identifies and forwards spam calls to voicemail, automated responses (“auto-replies”) for missed calls and texts, and voicemail-to-text transcription.
